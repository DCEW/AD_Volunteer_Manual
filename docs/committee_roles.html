Committee Roles

These roles are held continuously from one AGM until the next. They are proposed, seconded and elected by the committee at the AGM. The minimum tenure is therefore one year, except in exceptional circumstances.

The Chairperson, Treasurer and Secretary together constitute the “Executive Officers of the Club”. The committee must be made up of these three people, plus at least four other elected club members.

The Committee meets in person at least quarterly, in Alnwick. AGM’s are held in May or June annually.

We always welcome enthusiastic new recruits, and if you have some time to spare we can work with you to help you find a way to contribute. However, these roles also benefit from some continuity, and are often held for a number of years at a time.

Where relevant, Swim England provides guides to each of these roles, which you can find on their website, and are linked in each role page that follows.

What happens at an AGM?

The annual general meeting is a meeting of the whole club where the purpose is:

  • to receive the Chairman’s report of the activities of the Club during the previous year
  • to receive and consider the accounts of the Club for the previous year and the report on the accounts of the independent examiner and the Treasurer’s report as to the financial position of the Club
  • to remove and elect the independent examiner (who must not be a member of the Committee or a member of the family of a member of the Committee) or confirm that he/she remain in office
  • to elect the Executive Officers and other members of the Committee
